Raze RT -- credits and licences
===============================

What this disk image contains, who made it, and under what terms.

Raze (the game engine)
  The ZDoom team and contributors, after EDuke32, NBlood, Build (Ken Silverman) and
  GZDoom. GNU General Public License, version 2 or later.
  https://github.com/ZDoom/Raze
  Raze RT is Raze with the patches in Source/patches/raze. Source/setup.sh says which
  version of Raze they apply to and how it is built. Raze's Build code is also under the
  Build licence (buildlic.txt).

RTGL1 (the path tracer)
  Sultim Tsyrendashiev and contributors. MIT License.
  https://github.com/sultim-t/RayTracedGL1
  Raze RT's version is RTGL1 with the patches in Source/patches/rtgl1 (shaders) and
  Source/patches/rtgl1-lib (its Metal backend).

Libraries inside the engine (raze.app/Contents/Frameworks)
  OpenAL Soft (GNU LGPL 2), ZMusic (ZDoom team; GPL/LGPL/BSD parts), libsndfile (GNU
  LGPL 2.1), FLAC, Ogg, Vorbis, Opus (BSD licences). Their source is at their projects.

Duke-RT's lights and material maps (LIGHTOVR, material maps for Duke's textures)
  postmemetic and Duke-RT's contributors. https://github.com/postmemetic/Duke-RT

Raze RT's own files
  The material tables, Blood's window masks, the voxel definitions for Blood: Coagulated
  and the tuned settings were made for Raze RT. Same terms as Raze.

Not included (the installer takes them from the player's own copies)
  Duke Nukem 3D (3D Realms, Gearbox) and Blood (Monolith; Fresh Supply: Nightdive
  Studios): the games' data.
  Voxel Duke Nukem 3D and Blood: Coagulated (Cheello, Adam Matthews and others), the
  Blood Upscale Pack (Phredreeke and others), Death Wish and Marrow (their authors):
  download them from their pages (the Read Me has the links).
