Raze RT

Duke Nukem 3D and Blood with path-traced lighting, reflections, glass and volumetric smoke, running natively on Apple silicon Macs. Built on Raze and the RTGL1 path tracer.

You need: a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later; M3 or later recommended, because of their hardware ray tracing), macOS 14 or newer, and your own copy of the games. The games are not included.

1 Get the games' files

Raze RT plays the original games' data. Put each game's folder anywhere on your Mac, for example a Games folder in your home folder. The installer looks there, in Applications, in Steam's folder and in Downloads, and you can also point it at a folder yourself.

These releases are made for Windows. To get their files onto a Mac, you can copy the game's folder from a Windows PC where it is installed, or unpack the GOG installer with innoextract (brew install innoextract, then innoextract setup_….exe).

2 Optional: download the extras

These make the games look even better. They are made by other people, so download them yourself from their pages and leave them in your Downloads folder. The installer finds them there.

3 Install

  1. Open the disk image and double-click Install Raze RT.
  2. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app, because it isn't from the App Store or a registered developer. Click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to "Install Raze RT". Confirm with your password.
    Or, in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Volumes/Raze RT/Install Raze RT.app", then open it.
  3. The installer shows the games it found. Choose which to install, then which extras to take. If macOS asks whether it may read your Downloads folder, allow it.
  4. When it's done, you'll find Duke Nukem 3D RT and Blood RT in Applications.

4 Play

  • Open a game from Applications. Blood asks first whether to play Blood or an add-on (Cryptic Passage, Death Wish, Marrow).
  • The first time you play each level, its surface materials are made and kept, so the level can look plain for a few seconds.
  • Options → Ray Tracing has a quality preset (Low, Medium, High), the resolution and upscaling, dynamic resolution (it keeps the frame rate by lowering the render size), vertical sync, brightness and the effects. With dynamic resolution on, a lower preset gives a sharper picture at the same frame rate.
  • For the smoothest play, close or minimise busy windows such as a browser playing video: they share the graphics chip with the game.
  • The console opens with the key left of 1 (§ or `).

5 Where things are

Launchers/Applications (or ~/Applications)
Saves, settings~/Library/Application Support/Raze RT/Duke/user, …/Blood/user
Logs (if something goes wrong)…/Duke/logs, …/Blood/logs

Updating: run the new version's installer. Saves, settings and extras are kept.
Uninstalling: delete the launchers and the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Raze RT. Your game files are never changed.
